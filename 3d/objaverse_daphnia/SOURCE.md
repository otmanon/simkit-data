# objaverse_daphnia

**Daphnia** by tashtego

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/adb5de5332c5407f986495076e704ea0
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: taken at one frame of its animation; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; 4 small loose pieces dropped; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_daphnia.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_daphnia.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_daphnia_3d`.
