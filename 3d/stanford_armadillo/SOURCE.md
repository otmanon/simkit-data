# stanford_armadillo

**Armadillo** by Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory

- Source: The Stanford 3D Scanning Repository (Krishnamurthy and Levoy 1996), https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/
- Licence: Stanford 3D Scanning Repository terms
- Free to use for research and to redistribute for free, with credit to the Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory; not for commercial use without their permission.
- Changed from the original: rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `stanford_armadillo.obj`: the surface
- `stanford_armadillo.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `stanford_armadillo_3d`.
