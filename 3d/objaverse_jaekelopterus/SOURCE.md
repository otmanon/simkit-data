# objaverse_jaekelopterus

**Jaekelopterus** by Spore-Mantis

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/4ebef5af8ddb40aa9bdbc749ede09571
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 112 small loose pieces dropped; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_jaekelopterus.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_jaekelopterus.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_jaekelopterus_3d`.
