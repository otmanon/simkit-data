# objaverse_nudibranch

**Pikachu - The Sea Slug** by Leonth

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/5834d71aa2094944a557fca5ae123013
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_nudibranch.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_nudibranch.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_nudibranch_3d`.
