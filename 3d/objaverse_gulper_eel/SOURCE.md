# objaverse_gulper_eel

**Gulper Eel (Eurypharynx pelecanoides)** by SpaceGolby

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fbae0106acab48f4869141df9d8c37da
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_gulper_eel.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_gulper_eel.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_gulper_eel_3d`.
