# rigged_gorilla

**Proof of concept - Scifi Gorilla** by re1monsen

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/85ea87d33e7b47e490e3c3215457fbf1
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: read in its rest pose; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; 6 small loose pieces dropped; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `rigged_gorilla.obj`: the surface
- `rigged_gorilla.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `rigged_gorilla_3d`.
