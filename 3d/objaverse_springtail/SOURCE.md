# objaverse_springtail

**Springtail** by .hapto

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cc4dd8bc3c3f464a8d928cbf4518b0ef
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: taken at one frame of its animation; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; 136 small loose pieces dropped; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_springtail.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_springtail.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_springtail_3d`.
