# objaverse_nautilus

**Nautilus 3D Model** by Skache

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/3588500d9a9e410497f8cd97d9863ccb
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 6 small loose pieces dropped; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_nautilus.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_nautilus.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_nautilus_3d`.
