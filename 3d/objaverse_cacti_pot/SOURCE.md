# objaverse_cacti_pot

**Cacti pot** by Spacyy

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/71b53eaee72e4a829a9256a7bcfb7dab
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 643 small loose pieces dropped; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_cacti_pot.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_cacti_pot.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_cacti_pot_3d`.
