# objaverse_tick

**Tick** by ekgriffin

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/3523310bd5404108864bf215a104c387
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_tick.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_tick.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_tick_3d`.
