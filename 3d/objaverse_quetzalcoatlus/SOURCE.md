# objaverse_quetzalcoatlus

**Quetzalcoatlus** by seirogan

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/6e74bfadcce648048452150b52ca9b06
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 2 small loose pieces dropped; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_quetzalcoatlus.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_quetzalcoatlus.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_quetzalcoatlus_3d`.
