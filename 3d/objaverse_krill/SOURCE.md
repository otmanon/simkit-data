# objaverse_krill

**Game asset - Planktomania - Krill** by yannoch

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/f8a4d1ba05bc46ccaf32ed6cd7e40ce9
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: taken at one frame of its animation; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_krill.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_krill.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_krill_3d`.
