# objaverse_ichthyosaur

**ichthyosaur** by pro_alba

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/dffbc77b634a408f91dd5f68df4cc94f
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: taken at one frame of its animation; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_ichthyosaur.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_ichthyosaur.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_ichthyosaur_3d`.
