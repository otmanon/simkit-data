# objaverse_tapir

**Plastic Toy Tapir** by Arkify 3D

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/7249b80f6230410da3119cf24a1fc005
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_tapir.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_tapir.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_tapir_3d`.
