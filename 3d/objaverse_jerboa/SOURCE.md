# objaverse_jerboa

**jerboa from Poly by Google** by IronEqual

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/9044c4f6a5374abf956bbba0701b0960
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_jerboa.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_jerboa.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_jerboa_3d`.
