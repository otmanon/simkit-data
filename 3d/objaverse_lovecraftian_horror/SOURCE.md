# objaverse_lovecraftian_horror

**LOVECRAFTIAN HORROR** by alexalbinyana

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/6093cbb205444e9aa7b36d7be4ed9b58
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 75 small loose pieces dropped; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_lovecraftian_horror.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_lovecraftian_horror.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_lovecraftian_horror_3d`.
