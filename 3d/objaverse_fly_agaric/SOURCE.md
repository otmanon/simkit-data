# objaverse_fly_agaric

**Fly Agaric Mushroom** by Timothy Hsu

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/d6c3876f091b4de49a38553a177bab0f
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_fly_agaric.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_fly_agaric.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_fly_agaric_3d`.
