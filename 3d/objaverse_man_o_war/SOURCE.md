# objaverse_man_o_war

**Portuguese man o' war** by Shapelab

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cbd8c3e52f274ceeaae0872a70be5433
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_man_o_war.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_man_o_war.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_man_o_war_3d`.
