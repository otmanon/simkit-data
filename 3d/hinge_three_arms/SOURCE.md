# hinge_three_arms

**a T: 3 identical stiff arms, each hung on a light central post (the hub) by two square rods (knuckles) at its top and bottom edge, all turning about the post's axis z: one joint, 2 hinges**

Made for the modal_rigs project by `data/make_hinged_shapes_3d.py`.

- `hinge_three_arms.obj`: the surface
- `hinge_three_arms.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `hinge_three_arms_3d`.
