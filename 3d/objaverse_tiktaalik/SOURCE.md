# objaverse_tiktaalik

**Tiktaalik posed sculpted** by ricksticky

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/76f5ef700ffc431e966d85f4bc18512e
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_tiktaalik.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_tiktaalik.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_tiktaalik_3d`.
