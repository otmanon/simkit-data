# objaverse_aardvark

**Medium | Aardvark-Clay** by smeerws

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/4115e2452743435186a3e001ec2a0022
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 19 small loose pieces dropped; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_aardvark.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_aardvark.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_aardvark_3d`.
