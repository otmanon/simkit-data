# objaverse_dolphin_smooth

**Dolphin model (with easy texture)** by Sky4gj

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ece0cd544108424ba1e665db2149d74d
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_dolphin_smooth.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_dolphin_smooth.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_dolphin_smooth_3d`.
