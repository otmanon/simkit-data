# objaverse_venus_flytrap

**Flytrap with fangs covered in blood** by anyaachan

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/eec3c046107b489280ffead8b8fdc2c1
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_venus_flytrap.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_venus_flytrap.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_venus_flytrap_3d`.
