# objaverse_anomalocaris

**Anomalocaris 3D Model** by Skache

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/dad76e60589a41e18a42db1e979f81f8
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 34 small loose pieces dropped; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_anomalocaris.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_anomalocaris.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_anomalocaris_3d`.
