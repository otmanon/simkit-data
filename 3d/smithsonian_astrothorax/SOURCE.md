# smithsonian_astrothorax

**Astrothorax waitei** by Smithsonian Institution (NMNH)

- Source: Smithsonian 3D Digitization, https://3d-api.si.edu/voyager/3d_package:42fba0a6-68c4-45a1-ad08-92b243104ea9
- Licence: CC0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 2 small loose pieces dropped; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `smithsonian_astrothorax.obj`: the surface
- `smithsonian_astrothorax.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `smithsonian_astrothorax_3d`.
