# objaverse_dolphin

**Model 61A - Bottlenose Dolphin** by DigitalLife3D

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2ec20f15b08c4c2fb16e4df5d837b893
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_dolphin.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_dolphin.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_dolphin_3d`.
