# objaverse_orchid

**Orchid flower** by Lassi Kaukonen

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/75d19624c0d04b65975e011a04ae77a0
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_orchid.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_orchid.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_orchid_3d`.
