# rigged_bird

**Redcoat Robin Download** by runemarkstudio

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/6f81ad29c150407c84670c84d4f216c3
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: read in its rest pose; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; 5 small loose pieces dropped; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `rigged_bird.obj`: the surface
- `rigged_bird.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `rigged_bird_3d`.
