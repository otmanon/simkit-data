# objaverse_horseshoe_crab

**カブトガニ ♀ Horseshoe Crab, Tachypleus tridentatus** by ffish.asia / floraZia.com

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/581b1129a2f8494782c219fc36de9b82
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 6 small loose pieces dropped; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_horseshoe_crab.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_horseshoe_crab.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_horseshoe_crab_3d`.
