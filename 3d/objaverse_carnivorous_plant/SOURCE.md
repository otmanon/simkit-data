# objaverse_carnivorous_plant

**#14 "Carnivorous plant" Overgrown "Inktober2019"** by Canary Games

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/c85a1eeb606c4ba1b2941b61ddf3876a
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 43 small loose pieces dropped; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_carnivorous_plant.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_carnivorous_plant.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_carnivorous_plant_3d`.
