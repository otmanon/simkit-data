# rigged_mammoth

**Low Poly Mammoth** by rkuhlf

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/41cec47f5a354e15b8ea8d82e0e3a2fd
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: read in its rest pose; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `rigged_mammoth.obj`: the surface
- `rigged_mammoth.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `rigged_mammoth_3d`.
