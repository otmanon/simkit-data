# objaverse_dimetrodon

**Dimetrodon Dinosuar (photogrammetry)** by Austin Beaulier

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/c2f65e4e3fad4e199532ef79e6baea8d
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_dimetrodon.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_dimetrodon.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_dimetrodon_3d`.
