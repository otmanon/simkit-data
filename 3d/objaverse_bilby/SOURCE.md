# objaverse_bilby

**Bilby - animated** by nolanfa

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bbe258382f2f40f893a7f2effba1a6d4
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: taken at one frame of its animation; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; 8 small loose pieces dropped; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_bilby.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_bilby.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_bilby_3d`.
