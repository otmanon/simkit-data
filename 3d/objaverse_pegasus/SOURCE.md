# objaverse_pegasus

**Pegasus** by Alexei Ostapenko

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bc6026324be3415c8c083fadaddb2abe
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_pegasus.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_pegasus.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_pegasus_3d`.
