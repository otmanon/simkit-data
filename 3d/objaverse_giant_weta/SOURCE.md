# objaverse_giant_weta

**Giant Weta / Wētāpunga** by Auckland Museum

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/b1059de0b5e847a7870fd97ecc3d0b3a
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_giant_weta.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_giant_weta.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_giant_weta_3d`.
