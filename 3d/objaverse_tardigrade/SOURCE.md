# objaverse_tardigrade

**Water Bear** by oneillbeck

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/6e0ecc9d43ad4cf9a17efab2900f72ee
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_tardigrade.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_tardigrade.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_tardigrade_3d`.
