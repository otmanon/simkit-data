# objaverse_giant_isopod

**Giant Isopod** by RISD Nature Lab

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/46469c568b3743ec8fb4bb711949d49a
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 1 small loose pieces dropped; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_giant_isopod.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_giant_isopod.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_giant_isopod_3d`.
