# objaverse_cicada_nymph

**Cicada Shell** by Tyler Jackson

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/589f94f499bf45148fe453c11a3ec6b4
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 1 small loose pieces dropped; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_cicada_nymph.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_cicada_nymph.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_cicada_nymph_3d`.
