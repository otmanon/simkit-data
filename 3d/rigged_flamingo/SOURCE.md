# rigged_flamingo

**Dancing bird** by gareths11

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/1a054e81b94e4237b2851d918559bfae
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: read in its rest pose; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `rigged_flamingo.obj`: the surface
- `rigged_flamingo.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `rigged_flamingo_3d`.
