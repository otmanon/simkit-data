# objaverse_tentacled_creature

**Tentacled Creature (Sculpting Exercise)** by isdragonalex

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/9e621ee254d74bc2b6b1871bb4e038f8
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_tentacled_creature.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_tentacled_creature.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_tentacled_creature_3d`.
