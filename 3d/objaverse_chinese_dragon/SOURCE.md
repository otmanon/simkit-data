# objaverse_chinese_dragon

**Chinese Dragon** by icenvain

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/5f150cd861a245008045b89cdb6fbb7c
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 2 small loose pieces dropped; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_chinese_dragon.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_chinese_dragon.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_chinese_dragon_3d`.
