# rigged_lion

**Lion** by ShadowCoffee

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bee98906fe6c44c68b7eb0cfcbae8949
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: read in its rest pose; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; 26 small loose pieces dropped; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `rigged_lion.obj`: the surface
- `rigged_lion.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `rigged_lion_3d`.
