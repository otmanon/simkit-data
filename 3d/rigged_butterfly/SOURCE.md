# rigged_butterfly

**Butterfly** by EmmaDeBacker

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/65e674ef45dc46d5af584158082faf43
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: read in its rest pose; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `rigged_butterfly.obj`: the surface
- `rigged_butterfly.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `rigged_butterfly_3d`.
