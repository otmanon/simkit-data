# objaverse_tuatara

**Vertebrate: Tuatara (Sphenodon)** by Digital Atlas of Ancient Life

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/63637cfd29f04170ab758136976c4253
- Licence: CC0 1.0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_tuatara.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_tuatara.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_tuatara_3d`.
