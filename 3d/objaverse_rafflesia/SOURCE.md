# objaverse_rafflesia

**Rafflesia arnoldii flower** by saltoc in training

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/167b5be0f90c4184ad27e1c827ef88e4
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_rafflesia.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_rafflesia.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_rafflesia_3d`.
