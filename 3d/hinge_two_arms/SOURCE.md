# hinge_two_arms

**a bar: 2 identical stiff arms, each hung on a light central post (the hub) by two square rods (knuckles) at its top and bottom edge, all turning about the post's axis z: one joint, 1 hinge**

Made for the modal_rigs project by `data/make_hinged_shapes_3d.py`.

- `hinge_two_arms.obj`: the surface
- `hinge_two_arms.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `hinge_two_arms_3d`.
