# objaverse_desert_creature

**sci fi  desert creature** by Javier Martín Hidalgo

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/a510e91d2f91430f9798f6ec78e7f85a
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_desert_creature.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_desert_creature.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_desert_creature_3d`.
