# objaverse_mantis_shrimp

**シャコ Mantis shrimp, Oratosquilla oratoria** by ffish.asia / floraZia.com

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/5aa726fd3a0842e0b6e35681134ea2c5
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 13 small loose pieces dropped; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_mantis_shrimp.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_mantis_shrimp.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_mantis_shrimp_3d`.
