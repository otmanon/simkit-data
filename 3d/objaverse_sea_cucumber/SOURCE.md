# objaverse_sea_cucumber

**アカナマコ Sea Cucumber, Apostichopus japonicus** by ffish.asia / floraZia.com

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/6f011a5c78cf4a1296d537156bd2769b
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 1 small loose pieces dropped; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_sea_cucumber.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_sea_cucumber.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_sea_cucumber_3d`.
