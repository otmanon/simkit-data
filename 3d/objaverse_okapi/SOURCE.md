# objaverse_okapi

**Okapi (Okapia johnstoni)** by Museo [UV] Historia Natural

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/26a3c4a9bfdf4c8ca915d61a7f5a941e
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_okapi.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_okapi.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_okapi_3d`.
