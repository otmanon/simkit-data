# smithsonian_pycnopodia

**Pycnopodia helianthoides** by Smithsonian Institution (NMNH)

- Source: Smithsonian 3D Digitization, https://3d-api.si.edu/voyager/3d_package:0c69e749-b8df-4e5a-8d20-c38aebaf6279
- Licence: CC0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `smithsonian_pycnopodia.obj`: the surface
- `smithsonian_pycnopodia.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `smithsonian_pycnopodia_3d`.
