# objaverse_centipede

**トビズムカデ Centipede, Scolopendra mutilans** by ffish.asia / floraZia.com

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/456c8d2b022f4aaabf2fe843e872a282
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 2 small loose pieces dropped; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_centipede.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_centipede.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_centipede_3d`.
