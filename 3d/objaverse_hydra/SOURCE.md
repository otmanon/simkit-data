# objaverse_hydra

**Purple Hydra** by Lysa24

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/7976db7de7c149808522feb8c8a004a9
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_hydra.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_hydra.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_hydra_3d`.
