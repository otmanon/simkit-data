# rigged_frog

**Bomberman Frog** by zer0nim

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/102a2e5292844ab888a99ec85e23a42e
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: read in its rest pose; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `rigged_frog.obj`: the surface
- `rigged_frog.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `rigged_frog_3d`.
