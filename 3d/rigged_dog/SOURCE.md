# rigged_dog

**Puppy dog** by sidarthmenon

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/057dd0154cbc47549c73a092aa15199e
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: read in its rest pose; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; 65 small loose pieces dropped; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `rigged_dog.obj`: the surface
- `rigged_dog.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `rigged_dog_3d`.
