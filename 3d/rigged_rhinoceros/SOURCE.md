# rigged_rhinoceros

**Rhino animation** by mazormedia

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/0dd20f31ccfe46258fa2116f41b9d98c
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: read in its rest pose; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `rigged_rhinoceros.obj`: the surface
- `rigged_rhinoceros.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `rigged_rhinoceros_3d`.
