# rigged_goose

**Goose animation** by dinomaster

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/f36f865650a34f578780e0fcf95be99e
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: read in its rest pose; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; 2 small loose pieces dropped; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `rigged_goose.obj`: the surface
- `rigged_goose.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `rigged_goose_3d`.
