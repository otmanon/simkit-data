# objaverse_balloon_dog

**Balloon Dog** by Octaclee

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/e10d4f9e8eb34a62b1f67927e390d82f
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; rotated to stand upright; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_balloon_dog.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_balloon_dog.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_balloon_dog_3d`.
