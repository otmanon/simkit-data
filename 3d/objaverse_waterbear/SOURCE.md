# objaverse_waterbear

**Waterbear  - Christine Chung** by artsstucsd

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/8d2e53307b3344e387b4e9c31e926fef
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: taken at one frame of its animation; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_waterbear.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_waterbear.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_waterbear_3d`.
