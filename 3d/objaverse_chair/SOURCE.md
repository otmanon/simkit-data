# objaverse_chair

**Country style chair** by gerardusnl0

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/d601c2907f114376bf9826272d686e81
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_chair.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_chair.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_chair_3d`.
