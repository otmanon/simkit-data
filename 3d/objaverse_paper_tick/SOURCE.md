# objaverse_paper_tick

**Paper tick** by snot_nose

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/8b7ff3578e8645659f752747f304d49a
- Licence: CC BY 4.0
- Changed from the original: taken at one frame of its animation; surface decimated to 10,000 triangles; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_paper_tick.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_paper_tick.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_paper_tick_3d`.
