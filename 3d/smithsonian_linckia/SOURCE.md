# smithsonian_linckia

**Linckia laevigata** by Smithsonian Institution (NMNH)

- Source: Smithsonian 3D Digitization, https://3d-api.si.edu/voyager/3d_package:aff2c3e5-e909-4c18-87ce-8390bac19186
- Licence: CC0
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `smithsonian_linckia.obj`: the surface
- `smithsonian_linckia.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `smithsonian_linckia_3d`.
