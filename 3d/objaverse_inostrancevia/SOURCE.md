# objaverse_inostrancevia

**Inostrancevia alexandri** by Ilia

- Source: Objaverse 1.0 (Deitke et al. 2023), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/d0dcc6a25435468d861efbdc330b0238
- Licence: CC BY
- Changed from the original: surface decimated to 20,000 triangles; 50 small loose pieces dropped; centred and scaled to a unit bounding-box diagonal; its inside filled with tetrahedra.

- `objaverse_inostrancevia.obj`: the surface
- `objaverse_inostrancevia.mesh`: the tetrahedral mesh (MEDIT)

From the modal_rigs bundle `objaverse_inostrancevia_3d`.
